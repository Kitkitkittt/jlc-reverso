# Rights and provenance

## Original work

The original prose, data organization, scripts, procedural model and schematic artwork authored for this pack are licensed under LICENSE.md.

The schematics are independent explanatory graphics. They do not claim to be copied manufacturer CAD, authentic service plates or exact parts drawings.

Dial textures are original canvas drawings, not crops, AI alterations or reproductions of third-party photographs. They are provisional appearance approximations. The owner's source photograph remains byte-identical and is not embedded in model exports. Three.js is a separately licensed dependency; its license ships in the installed package.

Detailed movement meshes, perlage/brushing textures and guided assembly stages are original procedural educational work, not traced factory CAD or calibrated internal geometry. [Calibre assembled](renders/calibre-assembled.png), [calibre exploded](renders/calibre-exploded.png) and [calibre assembly MP4](renders/calibre-assembly.mp4) are renders of that original model. The MP4 contains no third-party footage or copied frames, and its sequence is explanatory rather than a service procedure.

## Owner reference photo

The unchanged owner-supplied wrist reference is retained privately and excluded from Git and public distribution. It is not required to run, build or validate a public clone. Rights remain with the original photographer/owner; no owner-photo license is granted. Public images are original model renders.

The public pack does not include guarantee certificates, serial numbers, private contact details or credentials.

## Third-party documents and imagery

Manufacturer manuals/catalogues, publisher imagery, seller photographs and original footage retain their respective rights. Public access does not by itself grant republication rights.

The repository uses external links and limited factual description. It does not bundle full third-party PDFs, seller snapshots, copied video frames or movement photographs.

The source-register locators document research provenance. Their presence does not authenticate a watch or grant rights to manufacture an identical product.

[S07's maintenance footage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BohN-EtjQuw) has been visually reviewed at recorded external locators, and the [S09 assembled photograph](references/movement-image-atlas.md) has been externally inspected with publisher attribution. Those reviews inform source-inspired artist geometry without importing media into the repository. S07's exact 854/1 engraving remains unread; source-title and publisher attribution do not authenticate the owner's unopened movement or establish calibrated measurements. Research links and factual observations do not transfer media rights.
