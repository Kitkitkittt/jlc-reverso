# Next evidence improvements

Only three evidence upgrades materially change the model now.

1. **Authenticated, calibrated 854/1 internal evidence.** S07 is now visually reviewed at recorded locators, with bridge removal, exposed train, barrel and running balance visible. Exact 854/1 engraving remains unread, so confirm compatibility, both faces and visible markings, then obtain calibrated dimensions and identifiable parts. S09's inspected publisher-attributed photo is not a trace or calibration. These upgrades determine whether the current source-informed geometry can become evidence-traced geometry.
2. **Straight-on reverse and control-side photographs.** These lock the lower 24-hour scale, hand profiles, crown/pusher shapes and exact dial textures.
3. **A small set of calibrated exterior measurements.** Confirm overall size convention, central moving-case dimensions, thickness, crown/pusher positions and bracelet opening.

A full history, pricing study or production-count estimate would not improve the exploded model at this stage.

## Unresolved geometry in the current app

The shipped exterior uses original procedural dial artwork and editable illustrative proportions. The numeral font, guilloche period, hand profiles, crown/pusher sizes, crystal properties and bracelet links are not calibrated reproductions. The overall catalogue envelope is not a movement measurement.

Release clearance and pivot placement are demonstration choices, not the watch's measured rail mechanism. Exploded gaps are presentation offsets, not removal instructions. The reverse lower scale uses an artistic zero orientation; its actual printing and day/night alignment await a sharp reference.

`M000` now contains twelve detailed educational groups, replacing the earlier opaque envelope. Mainplate, bridges, winding, barrel, train and balance are source-informed artist geometry; escapement/hairspring are general educational shapes; recto/reverse/24-hour works are schematic. [S07's reviewed footage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BohN-EtjQuw) and [the S09 image atlas entry](../references/movement-image-atlas.md) inform visual context, not owner authentication or calibrated internal measurements.

Exact movement footprint, bridge profiles, pivots, bores, tooth counts, fasteners and jewel locations remain unresolved. Clasp internals and retaining mechanisms also remain unsupported. The 12-second guided assembly in Calibre/Calibre exploded views is explanatory, not factory order or proof of physical fit. The 180 published components do not imply 180 meshes, 21 jewels do not specify the accent count, and the historical 45-hour reserve is not a measurement of the owner's watch. Evidence dimensions remain null where unmeasured; detailed geometry does not fill those unknowns.
