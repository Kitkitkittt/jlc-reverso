A case/model designation, commercial reference and individual serial number are different identifiers. The public model needs the first two. It does not need the owner's serial number or guarantee certificate.

## Specifications that can guide the model

S01 gives the Reverso Duo's hand winding, component count, frequency, reserve, display functions, one-hour pusher correction, steel variant and nominal case size. The reference index explicitly includes 271 81 10.

S02 and S03 directly catalogue other physical watches using 854/1 with 21 jewels. This is corroboration for the calibre family. It does not document an inspection of this owner's watch.

The catalogue dimension is an overall case convention, not a drawing with specified datum surfaces. Do not use 42.2 mm as the length of the moving central case, or 26 mm as the movement width.

## Historical context

The brand records the sliding and fully reversible case patent application in March 1931, and traces the Duoface concept to 1994, S05. These dates explain the design lineage. They do not establish the production year of this particular watch, its exact manufacture period or a total production count.

No price, investment claim or exclusivity estimate is needed for a geometric reference pack.
