## U03: supplied reverse presentation photograph

Inspected from the owner's previously supplied reference set. It is not rehosted because third-party publication rights have not been established.

Observations:

- The dial is dark grey to black, with a fine patterned field.
- The main hour/minute display is circular and sits in the upper part of the rectangular dial.
- Large Arabic quarter markers are visible at 12, 3 and 9 around that display, with slim intervening markers.
- A separate circular 24-hour display sits lower down, near the 6 position.
- DAY and NIGHT labels flank the lower display area.
- The hour and minute hands appear light/silver and have an inset luminous-looking treatment. Composition is not established by the photo.
- The crown and rectangular pusher are on the left when the reverse is upright toward the viewer.
- The pusher remains toward the 12 end relative to the crown.

Exact numeral placement on the lower 24-hour track, fonts, relief and minute marks should be checked against a close, straight-on reverse image before a final high-resolution texture is approved.

## Authority and contradictions

For appearance, follow these inspected photos over a dealer's stock description. S08, for example, calls the recto seconds display rectangular. The supplied photos show a circular seconds scale within a lower dial region. Use the visible geometry.

An AI image-search caption described an 854/1 image as having a micro-rotor. That conflicts with the manufacturer's manual-winding specification and is excluded. Image-search descriptions are discovery aids, not movement evidence.

A generic case sketch in the current attachment does not identify Q2718110 or 854/1. It is useful as a communication-style reference only.
