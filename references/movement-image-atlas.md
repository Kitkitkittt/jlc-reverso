# Movement and case image atlas

The atlas distinguishes an inspected assembled-movement photo, visually reviewed maintenance footage, a modern comparison and historical case imagery. These are not interchangeable, and none authenticates the owner's unopened movement.

## M01: image identified as 854/1

![External image identified as Jaeger-LeCoultre Calibre 854/1; assembled movement, not disassembled](https://cdn.luxuo.com/2016/07/Jaeger-LeCoultre-Calibre-854-1-movement-600x450.jpg)

- Source: S09, [publisher context](https://luxuothailand.com/watches/10-important-collector-watch-calibres/).
- Use: externally inspected assembled-movement appearance context for source-informed educational geometry.
- Status: publisher attribution recorded after external inspection. No bridge/pivot tracing or calibration performed.
- Scope: not the owner's opened watch, not an authenticated dimensional reference and not a disassembly atlas.
- Rights: external image, rights retained by its owners. No copy is stored in this repo.

Check the calibre engraving, face and stem direction directly before using the photo. Ignore the discovered AI caption's incorrect micro-rotor description.
