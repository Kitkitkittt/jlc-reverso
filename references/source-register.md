## Citation interpretation

S01 is original manufacturer content accessed through a mirror. The section's printed page number is used because a two-page-spread PDF may have a different viewer index.

S02/S03 provide firsthand cataloguing of other specimens with the same calibre. They cannot prove the owner's individual movement, dimensions or condition.

S06/S08 are direct seller records of specific watches. Their reported measurements can guide comparison, but an imprecise or mistaken description must not override visible evidence.

S07's title attributes Cal.854-1; selected video frames were inspected, but exact engraving is unreadable and reference compatibility is unconfirmed. The footage is nonorthographic and uncalibrated; it supports only the recorded view observations, not exact geometry, dimensions or an authenticated calibre identification.

S09 is a publisher-attributed assembled movement photo. The publisher identification is not owner service provenance or independent identity confirmation, and the image has no calibrated geometry.

S13 names Reverso Duoface and Grande Reverso Ultra Thin 1931 Duoface on printed pp.24–25, plus the Boutique Edition on p.26. The technical characteristics on p.27 state manually wound Calibre 854/1, 21,600 vibrations/hour and approximately 45 hours of reserve. Printed p.29 calls the setting subject the “Reverso Duo watch.” This strengthens historical family-level functional evidence, not exact-reference identification: the inspected text does not name Q2718110 or 272.8.54, and a supplied filename is not proof.

The booklet attributes its content to Jaeger-LeCoultre and carries the footer `MDE_ReversoDUOFACE_ALL_2013.indd` with production timestamp `9/10/12`. These identify the document family, not a verified publication/version date; PDF metadata indicates later processing, and original distribution provenance is unverified. The private file, its location and unrelated warranty/contact material are not published. No redistribution rights or calibrated geometry are inferred.

## Unresolved evidence

An exact-reference-confirmed Q2718110 historical user manual, authenticated 854/1 service/parts drawing, lubrication chart, full disassembly frame atlas, exact movement footprint and dimensioned bridge/gear geometry remain unavailable.

The pack deliberately leaves those absent items unresolved.
