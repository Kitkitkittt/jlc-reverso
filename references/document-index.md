# Technical and catalogue documents

## Historical manufacturer's catalogue

S01: [Collection 2015–2016](https://www.scribd.com/document/338020659/Jaeger-LeCoultre-JLC-Catalogue-2015-2016).

| Locator | What to inspect |
| --- | --- |
| Printed p.16, Reverso Duo | Calibre 854/1, display functions, pusher correction and nominal family dimensions |
| Printed p.149, Reverso collection reference index | Entry 271 81 10, stainless steel, 42.2 × 26 mm |

Printed page numbers come from the accessible catalogue text. Confirm the page index in a downloaded or licensed PDF rather than assuming it matches the printed number.

The catalogue is not a workshop manual. It supplies specification and product identity, not individual parts, tolerances or a disassembly sequence.

## Exact historical manual still needed

Use [JLC's manual finder](https://www.jaeger-lecoultre.com/mx-en/services/user-manual), S12, with the commercial reference. A general family manual is acceptable only if the dial functions and pusher arrangement match.

## Service drawing status

No public factory 854/1 exploded parts PDF was authenticated during this research. No service PDF is bundled. Do not title an original schematic “JLC technical drawing” or invent a PDF part number.

Only original documentation is included in the repository. External documents remain at their source URLs.
